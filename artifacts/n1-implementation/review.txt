N1 implementation — 2 November 2026

Scope: shared public-site typography, homepage, health library and detail pages,
doctor/service directories and profiles, visitor/feedback/referral pages,
appointment form, news/archive/article, emergency page, help and Patient Guide.
Fonts, content, heading semantics and routes are preserved.

Type roles (default root 16px; values are rem-based):
--site-small: 14px — secondary news metadata, partner captions, breadcrumbs,
compact utility navigation, guide speaker attribution and footer colophon.
--site-support: 16px — essential qualifications, image disclosures, care context,
search/privacy hints, form guidance/errors, labels and compact reading copy.
--site-body: 17px — standard prose.
--site-intro: 20px — introductory copy / emphasized text labels.
--site-label-heading: 24px — small component headings and field groups.
--site-lead: 28px desktop / 24px mobile — card headings and statement copy.
--site-subheading: 32px — utility section headings.
--site-content-heading: 32–40px — content section headings within task pages.
--site-heading: 36–58px — homepage section headings (42px tablet, 36px mobile).
--site-page-title: 40–72px — task-page titles.
--site-title: legacy large-title role retained for compatibility.
--site-hero: 78px desktop / 48px tablet / 42px mobile — homepage headline.

Presentation roles live in apps/web/src/app/styles.css, where the existing
public-site roles were defined. Form/search primitives use new component
support-font-size aliases in the canonical tokens.json, derived from the existing
16px semantic body role; generated CSS/TS are regenerated, not hand-edited.

Consolidation: repeated 13/14/15px essential notes become 16px; 18/19px lead copy
becomes 20px; 21/22/23px compact headings become 24px; 26/27px card headings join
the 28/24px role; equivalent fluid section and task-page headings share tokens.
Keep paragraph line heights around 1.5–1.7 and existing reading measures (typically
48–75ch). Tablet testimonial statements use 24px to avoid unnecessary long wraps.

Justified exceptions: hospital wordmarks, statistical display figures, clinician
monograms, alphabet initials, print-point sizes and icon glyphs retain their own
art direction. Legacy welcome rules remain overridden by the cinematic hero;
the active hero now scales in rem. Compact navigation/metadata are deliberately
14px, not essential instructions. Semantic heading levels are unchanged.
