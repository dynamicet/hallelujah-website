At the end the row resets to its start; this is a cycling carousel, not a continuously duplicated marquee.
Appointment destinations say Request an appointment, with doctor/service context where appropriate. Directory actions identify services, doctors or stories. News cards distinguish reading an article from viewing event details. The appointment step says Enter your details. Internal links use right arrows; content-choice, print and feedback-review controls no longer imply navigation. Existing hrefs and query parameters are preserved.
