# Institution-Specific Benchmark Candidate Crosswalk

**Prepared:** 11 September 2026  
**Status:** All 120 institution-specific candidates mapped and approved unchanged through `B00-017` / `GOV-03` on 13 September 2026  
**Parent:** [Benchmark traceability and local incorporation](benchmark-traceability.md)

## Purpose

The parent register makes product decisions at the synthesized cross-pattern level. This appendix proves that every institution-specific candidate in the benchmark source pack is accounted for individually and routes to the same local application, gate, and approval model.

`Adopt`, `Adapt`, `Test`, and `Defer` describe the approved benchmark-use boundary for controlled implementation only. They do not approve hospital facts, workflows, content, technology, or release scope. Candidate wording remains authoritative in the linked source documents; this appendix records the local disposition without reproducing the benchmark analysis. Apply the parent register's [change-triggered review rule](benchmark-traceability.md#b00-017-approval-record).
