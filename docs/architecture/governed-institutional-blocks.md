# Governed Institutional Content Blocks

B02-008 adds an institutional-block kind to the existing authenticated governed-record collection.
The [execution brief](../backlog/b02-008-governed-institutional-blocks.md) limits work to synthetic
local contracts and internal consumers. INST-01–INST-03 remain unresolved.

Narrative, mission, leadership, milestone, fact and relationship records each carry one claim/block
and its source, definition, limitations and as-of/review/expiry instants. Numeric and superlative
text cannot bypass evidence by using a generic narrative: all included types require the same
evidence. Stable opaque identity and type cannot change. One current mission prevents competing
mission text; reconciliation requires retiring the competing record explicitly.

Independent content review stamps evidence; content, brand and legal reviews plus general approval
use the existing lifecycle. Authoring cannot supply stamps and returning to draft clears them.
The content role is a synthetic executive/content contract, not executive approval. Existing
PostgreSQL advisory locking enforces complete-set conflicts, revisions and atomic rollback.

Leadership records state name/role without a portrait. Relationships explicitly distinguish partner
from insurer and withhold coverage. Localized body text expresses patient-relevant meaning under
the review contract; a partner logo or name never creates coverage eligibility.

The authenticated internal consumer checks live persisted review/approval identities under the
write lock. Effective publication, record review/expiry and earlier evidence review/expiry all
control omission. Missing translations create no empty block. Public eligibility denies even
non-synthetic institutional records. The semantic specimen escapes all text, displays as-of,
definitions and limitations, and labels Amharic as unreviewed shaping. No route is mounted.
