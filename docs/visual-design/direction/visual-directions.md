# Order 2 Visual Directions and Recommendation

**Prepared:** 12 September 2026  
**Status:** Direction selected for non-public prototyping; `BRAND-01` and all production gates remain unresolved  
**Rendered companion:** [Direction studies](studies.html)

## 1. Shared brief

All three directions preserve the accepted page hierarchy and are designed for the same experience character: calm clinical confidence, locally human. They must support urgent access, doctor/service discovery, appointment requests, practical arrival, institutional trust, English/Amharic geometry, 320 px reflow, keyboard focus, reduced motion, and useful no-image/no-font/no-JavaScript behavior.

The local design-intelligence search suggested an exaggerated-minimalist style and Atkinson Hyperlegible. The oversized typography and English-only font recommendation were not adopted: oversized display treatment would compete with essential tasks, and Atkinson has no verified Ethiopic companion within the project. Its accessibility intent is retained through large readable body text, strong contrast, restrained measures, and the candidate Noto family.

## 2. Shared non-negotiables

- Persistent visible label: “Non-public prototype — content, contacts, brand, and operations are placeholders and are not hospital-approved.”
- Text-only prototype identity; no invented symbol, traced logo, tagline, or logo colors.
- Red `#B42318` is reserved for emergency and error meaning, which remain distinct through words, structure, and iconography.
- One H1, sentence case, underlined inline links, 16 px minimum body, approximately 18 px opening guidance, and 45–75 character prose measure.
- 44 × 44 px minimum targets, 8 px separation, a 3 px focus outline with 2 px offset, and no hover-only meaning.
- Page content works without imagery; benchmark images appear only in the research strip, never in interface fragments.
- Icons are neutral 2 px outline studies paired with visible text. A final licensed/self-hosted family is an Order 4 decision.
- No live contact, form, map, analytics, remote font, or third-party request.
- Amharic strings are visibly labeled as unreviewed geometry fixtures; neither hierarchy nor wrapping proves translation quality.

## 3. Direction A — Calm Wayfinding (recommended)

### Moodboard

**Words:** assured, clear, breathable, grounded, humane, navigable.  
**Reference qualities:** NHS public-service directness; CCAD/Sidra bilingual visibility; AKU regional human relevance; text-first arrival and governance from the local blueprint.  
**Original expression:** deep teal action color, mineral-cyan quiet surfaces, a small sun-warm ochre accent, rounded-but-disciplined shapes, topographic/wayfinding line motifs, and photography reserved for real people/place context only.

This direction treats the interface like a well-marked path through an unfamiliar place. Strong rules and step markers make actions obvious; rounded corners and a warm accent prevent the system from feeling bureaucratic. Whitespace is purposeful rather than luxurious.

These arithmetic ratios validate candidate solid pairs only. Rendered font, anti-aliasing, adjacent color, disabled states, gradients, and opacity remain Order 4/11 checks.

### Typography, imagery, icon, and layout study

- **Type:** `Noto Sans` for Latin and `Noto Sans Ethiopic` for Ethiopic, weights 400/600/700. A unified family gives the best chance of comparable x-height, stroke, and hierarchy. Self-hosting, license files, subsets, loading, and professional language review remain unresolved.
- **Imagery:** future authentic staff, entrance, reception, and neighborhood/landmark details in calm natural light. Crops show useful context, never procedures, distress, or implied outcomes. Until rights approval, an abstract line-and-field placeholder occupies geometry only.
- **Icons:** rounded/neutral outline, 20 px in controls and 24 px for support. Icon plus label is mandatory for care, emergency, call, directions, and language actions.
- **Shape:** 8 px controls, 12 px cards/media, 1 px default border, 2 px emphasis. Warm accent appears as a short rule or step marker rather than a large fill.
- **Rhythm:** 16 px compact gutter; 32 px mobile and 64 px desktop section separation; 24 px component padding; no card around continuous prose.
- **Layout:** bounded 1120 px standard container, 720 px reading measure, single-column emergency and appointment, 1/2/maximum 3 result columns.

### Representative studies

The rendered board shows `H1` Home orientation, `E1` emergency call choice, `D4` doctor card with neutral non-person fallback, `A3/A4` appointment error/field, `LC3/LC5` text-first arrival, and `AB2/AB4/AB8` evidence-gated trust content. These fragments use controlled placeholder labels, not publishable copy.

## 4. Direction B — Warm Civic Care

### Moodboard

**Words:** welcoming, communal, practical, familiar, steady, tactile.  
**Reference qualities:** AKU's regional human context; Kaiser's approachable task prompts; NHS plain service language; local before/during/after structure.  
**Original expression:** warm paper surface, forest-green action color, ochre indexing, larger section numerals, light editorial rules, and place-led imagery.

This direction feels closest to a friendly public-service guide. It is strongest for visit preparation and institutional story, but the warmer surfaces and editorial markers require more discipline to keep emergency/error red unmistakable and to avoid drifting into campaign styling.

### Typography, imagery, icon, and layout study

- **Type:** Source Sans-style humanist Latin paired with Noto Sans Ethiopic for the concept. The pairing feels welcoming but introduces a metrics/parity risk and a second-family delivery cost; it is not recommended over unified Noto.
- **Imagery:** entrances, staffed help, caregiver arrival, and neighborhood cues; slightly warmer natural color. No staged consultation, patient vulnerability, or unverified hospital interior.
- **Icons:** rounded outline with small indexed circles. Labels remain primary.
- **Shape:** 6 px controls, 4 px/10 px mixed surface corners, visible editorial rules, no decorative shadow.
- **Rhythm:** more compact within task groups, generous breaks between visit stages; large numbers support sequences but never replace headings.
- **Layout:** slightly asymmetric wide compositions, always returning to DOM order at zoom; emergency and forms remain centered single columns.

## 5. Direction C — Quiet Clinical Precision

### Moodboard

**Words:** exact, structured, clinical, dependable, restrained, efficient.  
**Reference qualities:** Charité's separated finders; Mediclinic's search prominence; Kaiser record/task clarity; local governed metadata.  
**Original expression:** graphite typography, deep blue actions, eucalyptus secondary emphasis, square geometry, stronger grid lines, and highly structured metadata.

This direction maximizes scanning and governance visibility. It is strong for directories, forms, and detailed content, but feels less locally human and can resemble a generic enterprise/clinical system if photography is absent.

### Typography, imagery, icon, and layout study

- **Type:** Atkinson Hyperlegible for Latin paired with Noto Sans Ethiopic as an accessibility-led experiment. Because the project has no approved/bundled Atkinson files and no proof of cross-script metrics, the study falls back to Noto/system fonts and remains comparative only.
- **Imagery:** infrequent documentary details with near-neutral grading; record text does not depend on them.
- **Icons:** neutral geometric outline, square containers, labels always visible.
- **Shape:** 4 px controls and cards, stronger 1–2 px grid lines, no shadow.
- **Rhythm:** denser record groups, explicit metadata zones, 24/48 px section rhythm.
- **Layout:** highly aligned split panes where readable; immediate collapse to one column at content-fit thresholds.

## 7. Recommendation

Proceed with **Direction A — Calm Wayfinding** as the working direction for Orders 3 and 4.

It wins because:

1. Deep teal provides a strong non-emergency action color while leaving red visually concentrated for genuine urgency.
2. The unified Noto direction minimizes cross-script hierarchy drift and custom-font dependency.
3. Its wayfinding metaphor supports emergency access, arrival, care discovery, and form recovery without inventing clinical symbolism.
4. Warmth comes from a small accent, humane spacing, and future authentic place/staff imagery—not promotional claims or image dependence.
5. Its borders, quiet surfaces, restrained radii, and no-shadow baseline remain readable and performant on small or low-cost devices.
6. It is sufficiently distinct from the benchmark screenshots: no borrowed logo geometry, signature color block, hero composition, navigation model, or copy.

Keep Direction B as the fallback if hospital review asks for more warmth and place-led expression. Keep Direction C as a reference for data-dense directories and governance metadata, not as the sitewide character.

## 8. Order 3 handoff

Order 3 should explore a restrained wayfinding motion language for Direction A: short path continuity, focus/state transitions, and section orientation. Emergency `E1` and other safety-critical content must render immediately, remain static, and be fully understandable with no animation. Reduced motion removes spatial travel and preserves direct state changes. The Order 3 study must not turn the line motif into scroll-drawn decoration that delays content or increases initial transfer materially.
