# Epic 02 — CMS, Content Governance, and Assets

**Outcome:** Payload CMS models and workflows that publish only approved, current, traceable, bilingual content; safely omit missing content; and withdraw expired records from every surface.

## Epic exit criteria

- CMS roles and workflows enforce governance rather than relying on editorial memory.
- Every public record has stable identity, source, owner, approval state, review/expiry, localization, and retirement behavior.
- Content migration is reconciled and repeatable; source evidence is never treated as automatic approval.
- Withdrawing a record removes it from pages, relationships, search, sitemaps, caches, and assets according to policy.

## Explicit exclusions

- No hotlinking or reuse of audit, benchmark, old-site, or generated evidence imagery as production assets.
- No WYSIWYG freedom to introduce arbitrary scripts/styles or bypass structured governance.
- No schedule, capability, partner/insurer, leadership, or numerical claim without its specific approval record.
