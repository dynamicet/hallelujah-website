**Outcome:** A trusted, reproducible, least-privilege delivery foundation that can build and test controlled implementation slices without implying approval to collect data or publish the site.  
**Priority:** P0  
**Milestones:** M0–M1  
**Primary owners:** Hospital Project Owner, Hospital IT/Security Manager, External Delivery Lead, QA/Accessibility Lead

The live execution board records scoped deferrals for `B00-014` and `B00-015`. Dependent local development may proceed only under its default-deny third-party and sanitized-evidence baselines. This does not satisfy the tickets' full acceptance criteria: they return in Phase 2 or earlier before an external-provider request/activation, restricted-evidence workflow, or production acceptance boundary is crossed. The board records the working [implementation acceptance matrix](../validation/implementation-acceptance-matrix.md) as complete; `B00-016` is deferred only for named authorities, actual version/device/repository records, executed release-candidate evidence, and production authorization. The board also records `B00-017` as Done: the benchmark crosswalk is approved unchanged for controlled implementation and returns only on its documented change triggers; referenced domain gates remain authoritative.

## Epic exit criteria

- `B00-001`, `B00-002`, `B00-015`, and `B00-016` have named, dated evidence.
- A clean checkout builds and runs locally and in CI from pinned dependencies.
- Environment, database, secret, and evidence boundaries are demonstrably enforced.
- Backup restore and deployment rollback are rehearsed in a non-production environment.
- Appointment collection and all optional third parties remain disabled until their feature-specific gates close.

## Explicit exclusions

- No migration from an untrusted source.
- No real appointment data in local or staging.
- No automatic production deployment.
- No provider approval merely because a library or service is technically convenient.
