# Epic 03 — Critical Journeys

**Outcome:** Safe, resilient Home, Emergency, Contact, and Appointment journeys that remain truthful and usable on small screens, slow networks, without JavaScript, and in the approved Phase 1 language scope.  
**Priority:** P0/P1  
**Milestones:** M2 and M5  
**Primary owners:** Product, Frontend, Backend, Operations, Clinical, Privacy/Security, QA/Accessibility

## Epic exit criteria

- Home routes users to every essential task without relying on promotional content.
- Emergency calls and written arrival guidance use current approved records and pass live human/device checks.
- Appointment collection is impossible until the full approved server path is enabled.
- Every appointment result is authoritative, duplicate-safe, privacy-bounded, and operationally reconcilable.
- Staging-complete, content-approved, collection-enabled, and publication-approved are recorded independently.

## Explicit exclusions

- No diagnosis, urgency estimation, chatbot, unstaffed emergency messaging, or inferred public-emergency number.
- No confirmed-booking language unless an operational system actually confirms the booking.
- No client-only appointment persistence or success state.
