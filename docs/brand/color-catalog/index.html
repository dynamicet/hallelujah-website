How the whole site holds together

Compose the layers together.

Choose the reading ground, text, actions and decorative detail independently. Blue gives continuity; orange and magenta add expression through type, lines, frames, shapes and surfaces. Paper and white give each task room to read. There are no fixed color percentages, exclusive section colors or one-accent limits.

A peach plane can carry blue headings and a magenta marker. A deep-blue field can hold white content, pale tonal shapes and a warm directional cue. Let hierarchy and the visitor’s task decide the balance.

Give each shade a reason.

  • Primary blue: main action and selected identity moments.
  • Strong blue: underlined links on white, paper, mist and veil.
  • Deep blue: headings, pressed states and inverse fields.
  • Mist and veil: tonal grouping. Air: decorative fill with deep-blue text.
  • Orange and magenta: independent supporting detail and composed surfaces, with readable pale/deep companions.
  • Green, amber and red: actual states with written meaning.
