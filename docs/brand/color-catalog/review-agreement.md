## Agreed decisions

Brand Guardian, UI Designer, and UX Architect explicitly agreed on these rules following the five-role review. The Accessibility Auditor reviewed the completed study and mapping without a source-proven blocker. The Visual Storyteller's recommendations informed accent placement and photography treatment.

- Preserve the anchor hues. Primary blue carries filled actions, strong blue ordinary links, and deep blue headings and inverse fields. Reading ink remains separate.
- Use white fields with the control edge in the supported light contexts. Quiet borders are decorative. Do not use the control edge against blue veil as the sole necessary boundary: 2.9936:1 fails the 3:1 threshold.
- Orange supports visit guidance through deep-orange text on pale orange. Anchor orange is not an ordinary-text surface for white or reading ink.
- Magenta appears occasionally in editorial content, without gender, condition, specialty, urgency, or validation meaning.
- Keep urgent access and validation errors separate in wording, structure, and semantic aliases even where they share red.
- Preserve natural photography and evaluate image scrims separately. The study reuses the existing disclosed concept scene; it is not evidence of actual patients, staff, or hospital facilities.
- Preserve the site's type families and component/composition patterns. The standalone study is a representative specimen, not a pixel-identical replacement for every live component.

## Validation and remaining limits

Browser checks use the actual file URLs at 320, 390, 768, and 1440 pixels. They cover horizontal overflow, image loading, fragment destinations, JavaScript errors, local form validation and reset, urgent-role disclosure, and a forced-colors hero fallback. Desktop, tablet, and mobile screenshots are inspected. The browser profile is kept in the workspace because the system temporary filesystem produced quota errors.

Review evidence and the reproducible browser check are in `.taste-skill/color-role-review/`; these are local review artifacts. The existing brand-foundation and repository local-link checks also cover the documentation update.

The marketing-page inspector reports rules such as missing motion, few photographs, and form labels mistaken for eyebrows. Those rules are not acceptance requirements for a color reference or component specimen. This work preserves the curated palette and practical controls rather than adding unrelated imagery or animation to satisfy that meter.

This review does not certify full WCAG conformance. Screen-reader announcements, image-dependent text contrast across all future crops, and the complete application cascade need verification during adoption. The checklist in the mapping remains a gate for the later website migration.
