Adaptations to the dealt sheet

The open-arm curve from the selected motif direction takes precedence.
Object-on-disc showcases and isolated product specimens do not represent hospital
services; replaced by the supplied references' care-discovery and people layouts.
A coupon contact form would conflict with the existing appointment journey.
The real GET search and existing appointment flow provide the working actions.
No unrelated stock photographs, invented statistics, partner logos, testimonials,
operating hours, clinical claims or hospital contact values were added.
