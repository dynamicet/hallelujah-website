Hallelujah Hospital redesign — 27 October 2026

Preview: http://localhost:3000 (existing Next development server).

Direction: Taste “The place carries it”, seed 2146993501.
Idea: The hope in Hallelujah becomes a photographic welcome followed by a
practical guide to the next step. The interactive care navigator connects to
doctors, services, visit preparation and the appointment journey.

Delivered:
- A new photographic homepage, service pathways, listening/approach section,
  native visit-preparation accordions, honest arrival information and a large
  typographic footer.
- Shared responsive header, keyboard-operable native mobile menu, visible
  emergency access and a language-availability disclosure.
- Refined doctor and service directories, compact clinician monograms and
  profiles, a consistent appointment presentation and urgent-care styling.
- Locally hosted Clash Grotesk, optimized local WebP imagery, progressive
  enhancement, reduced-motion and print treatments.
- Twenty additional canonical presentation tokens and six additional contrast
  policies. Generated CSS and TypeScript remain in sync.
- Updated connected-route regression tests for the care navigator, no-JavaScript
  fallback, visitor questions and clinician initials.

Direction adjustments:
The original midline navigation needed a photograph with a clear central rail.
That asset did not exist; the hero was redrawn. The type slot was redrawn from
condensed Archivo to Clash Grotesk for a calmer, readable identity. The dealt
multi-screen pinned introduction was adapted to the practical care navigator;
the hospital’s essential routes remain near the first screen. No pinned scene
or scroll hijacking was introduced.

Assets and credits:
The three generated care scenes are stored in apps/web/public/editorial:
hope-care.webp, family-care.webp and together-care.webp. Generated with the
built-in OpenAI image tool, with fictional people and settings. The second
family placement is a different crop of the same image. No external stock
photographs, archival images, video or code-drawn photo substitutes were used.
The hero, supporting caption and footer disclose concept imagery. Full prompts,
font sources and asset descriptions are in public/editorial/CARE-IMAGERY.txt.
The hospital’s existing emblem is preserved and supplied explicitly by the
connected website shell. Clinician initials do not imply an available portrait.

Launch limitations:
The connected demonstration remains development-only under the application’s
existing deployment boundary. The ready local homepage uses the new design;
the existing production homepage continues to use its governed-content template
with the shared visual theme. Alternative local scenarios are retained.
Clinician and service records are the existing illustrative snapshot, not
verified clinical information. Phone, address, entrance, hours, capabilities,
availability and emergency operational details require hospital verification.
The appointment preview does not submit or retain patient data. No live booking,
map, telephone or Amharic content was invented or activated. This work has not
been deployed or published.

For launch, supply approved operational content and portraits, replace concept
scenes with authentic institutional photography where possible, connect the
