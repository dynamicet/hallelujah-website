Argument and copy
Hallelujah Hospital helps visitors find a doctor and prepare for a visit.
The first screen states the hospital name and gives a working doctor search.
The directory provides the supportable detail: specialties and languages.
Headline: Care with open arms.
Support: Hallelujah Hospital. Find a doctor, explore care, and plan your visit.
Primary action: Find a doctor / Name or specialty / Search.
Secondary action: Browse doctor profiles.
Supporting detail: Specialties and languages, before you visit.

Idea: the open arms of the emblem frame a welcoming family scene and an
immediate route to care. Live interaction: the existing GET doctor search.
No new imagery, fonts, dependencies, factual claims or external services.

Changes and measurement
The hospital name leads the supporting sentence. Search and supporting copy no
longer wait for a reveal animation. The search button retains its visible word
label on tablets and phones. The shorter placeholder fits the narrower input.
The directory link describes specialties and languages. The decorative photo
caption was removed; its AI provenance is now the semantic figure caption.
Desktop retains the two-column composition. Phones show the image first.
At 390x844, the image starts at 179px instead of about 583px, and the search
button ends at about 659px. At 320x740, search ends at about 620px.
All measured hero headings remain two lines. Hero text fits its copy column at
320, 360, 390, 599, 600, 768, 1024 and 1440px. The search is on the first screen
at all tested viewport heights; touch targets are at least 44px high.
