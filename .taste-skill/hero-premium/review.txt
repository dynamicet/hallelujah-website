Hallelujah General Hospital — hero evaluation and implementation
28 October 2026

Verdict
The original was visually confident but communicated family warmth more
strongly than clinical care. Large type and a useful search were worth keeping.
The change makes the consultation the visual argument and gives appointment
requests a clear place in the hierarchy. Hospital-specific photography and
verified institutional evidence remain the biggest opportunity for distinction.

Original hero assessment and decisions
Hierarchy: the promise and search dominated; appointment access was visually
secondary. The original source did include a plain appointment link below
search, while its mobile header action was hidden in the menu. Promote the
appointment to the only filled action in the main scene.

Composition: an equal-looking dark panel/family portrait split felt familiar.
Replace it with one integrated photographic stage. Keep faces free of type and
retain one emblem-inspired open corner as the identifying framing detail.

Typography: the existing Clash Grotesk/Lato pairing and two-line title were
strong. Keep both; shorten the promise to “Care begins with you.” Set the
desktop title at roughly 98px, tablet at 64px and mobile at 52px. Keep supporting
copy at 16–19px, with the hospital name explicit.

Imagery: a mother-and-daughter courtyard portrait communicated welcome but
provided little clinical context. Reuse the existing consultation concept
image in the hero. Move the family scene to visit preparation, avoiding a
repeated hero image later on the page. Keep the AI disclosure visible.

Spacing: the original search and secondary links crowded the message field.
Group headline, short supporting paragraph and appointment button with 28px
gaps on desktop. Give search its own pale band below the scene, with simple
service and visit links. No floating panel obscures the consultation.

Motion: replace the sixteen-second image drift with a 1.2-second settling
movement. The heading, copy and action paint immediately and remain static. No
carousel, looping decorative motion or scroll lock. Reduced motion disables
animations and interaction travel.

CTA placement: the main appointment button now sits immediately below the
promise at every width. Doctor search is a separate secondary task. Remove
the redundant browse-profiles link: submitting the empty search still opens
the directory. Keep services and visit preparation as quiet text links.

Add later, when supplied: approved photographs of Hallelujah's own team and
consultations, plus one verified reason to choose the hospital, such as a
specific clinical service or location. No rankings, outcome statistics,
accreditations, operating hours or contact details were invented.

Direction
Dealt: “The place carries it”, seed 1396877447. Existing brand type and palette
were retained. One hero redraw was recorded because the initial sky/horizon
blueprint required an image with empty sky above its subjects; the available
consultation has its clear space on the left. The implementation adapts the
photographic-stage principle to that real asset. The dealt moving-line detail
was omitted in favor of a brief transform/opacity image entrance, appropriate
to the healthcare setting. The task band sits outside the image, preserving
the scene. This is a hero-only implementation, not a page-wide rebuild of the
dealt section blueprints.

Subject inventory
Hallelujah name; General Hospital designation; existing open-arms emblem;
the emblem's blue palette; existing deep teal/mint presentation tokens;
Ethiopian people in the illustrative imagery; the consultation scene's eye
contact and conversational gesture; the directory's specialty/language fields;
the request-based appointment journey; the existing visit guide. Unverified
address, rankings and institutional numbers were excluded as proof.
